   - Call `get_symbol_overview` on main.go, assert still contains "Helper" (name unchanged, body changed)

   - Call `read_file` on main.go, assert contains "AFTER_MARKER"
   - Assert BEFORE_MARKER appears before "func Helper" and AFTER_MARKER appears after the Helper function body

3. `TestEdit_RenameCrossFile(t *testing.T)` — EDIT-03

   - Verify UsingHelper's body now calls RenamedHelper (cross-reference updated)

4. `TestEdit_SafeDelete(t *testing.T)` — EDIT-04
