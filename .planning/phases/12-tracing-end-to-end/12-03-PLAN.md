<objective>
Give the forwarder process its own `obs.Provider` (noop-only for v1.2) so that (a) the gRPC client's `otelgrpc.NewClientHandler` has an explicit TracerProvider to attach to — honoring D-01 no-global rule — and (b) the 3-span tree (`forwarder.tools.call` → `daemon.mcp.tools.call` → `kernel.tool.{name}`) has a real root created in the forwarder process.

Purpose: addresses Pitfall 3 (Forwarder Has No Provider) and delivers the client-side half of TRACE-01. This was missed in CONTEXT.md and surfaced by research; this plan is the correction.

Output: forwarder main flow constructs Provider, dial.go installs client stats handler, a minimal root span wraps outgoing tools/call forwards. Forwarder remains a thin stdio-to-gRPC proxy — no OTLP endpoint config, no sampler tuning (noop tracer drops everything, and the daemon's exporter catches spans via gRPC traceparent propagation).
</objective>
