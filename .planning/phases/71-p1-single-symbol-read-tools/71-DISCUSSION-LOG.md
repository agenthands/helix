## Area 3 — `validate_graph_edge` Evidence Shape

**Options presented:**
- A: flat list, closed-enum source, lenient (Recommended)
- A-strict: same but zero evidence → zero confidence
- B: tree of derivation

**Selected:** A (lenient).

**Rationale:** Flat list with `source ∈ {lsp, ast, type_resolver}`, cap 10,
sorted by `confidence_contribution`. Type-resolver citations carry `tier`
field reusing the existing 7-tier enum in `internal/semantic/types/`.
`evidence_status ∈ {complete, partial, none}` populated independently of
confidence. Phase 62 TYPES-04 confidence cap (≤ 0.6) applies on degraded
paths.
