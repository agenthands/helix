<objective>
Wire the no_lsp ablation arm at the daemon composition root: add the two CLI
override flags (`--disable-lsp-subsystem`, `--disable-structured-edit-subsystem`)
and their `SerenaConfig` fields, compute effective flags with CLI-over-profile-over-
default-off precedence (D-02/D-03), and — under `disable_lsp_subsystem` —
structurally suppress every LSP-touching seam via null-object injection (D-09):
no-op/absent `EditNotifier`, skipped `SetEnrichFn`, and the D-10-audited
pool-leasing repomap-fallback + diag `leaseFn` paths neutralized so they cannot
acquire a live lease. Prove the result with a trace-tap that asserts ZERO
`lspool.lsp.*` spans (ABLATE-05, ROADMAP success criterion #2 hard fail).

Purpose: This is the only structurally-invasive wiring change in the milestone.
Achieving no_lsp by what the daemon does NOT wire — not by scattered per-callsite
flag checks — makes the zero-span guarantee hold by construction and keeps the
leakage surface that `vet-ablation-leakage` exists to catch from growing.

Output: 2 CLI flags + their override-map entries; 2 SerenaConfig fields;
effective-flag computation + conditional null-object wiring in daemon.go;
TestNoLSPWiring + TestNoLSPZeroSpans.

Depends on Plan 76-01 (kernel.KernelConfig.DisableLSPSubsystem field +
LSPSubsystemDisabled() accessor) and Plan 76-02 (Profile.DisableLSPSubsystem
field read from the resolved bench-no-lsp profile).
</objective>
