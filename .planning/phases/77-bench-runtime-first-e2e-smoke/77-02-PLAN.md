<objective>
Build the two pure-transform glue pieces the cell orchestrator needs: the `result.v2.json` builder
(NONE exists today — Pitfall 2) that produces a schema-valid, provenance-complete / metric-sparse
doc (D-04), and the CC-tap synthesizer (D-02) that turns the scripted agent's executed
`[]runner.StepResult` into a `trace.CCTapResult` so the hermetic smoke asserts a real 2-leg merge.

Purpose: These are TDD candidates with crisp input->output contracts and no subprocess/IO, so they
get their own RED/GREEN/REFACTOR plan, decoupled from the daemon-drive integration spine (Plan 03)
which consumes them. Splitting them out keeps both the builder and the synth at peak quality.

Output: `bench/runtime/result.go` (+ test) and `bench/runtime/cctap.go` (+ test).
</objective>
