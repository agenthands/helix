<objective>
Lay the foundation every Phase 79 grader and the runtime wiring depend on: the typed nullable `Metrics` struct (+ `MetricError`) in Go, and the additive schema typing of all 17 metrics under a `metrics` object plus a `metric_errors[]` array in `result.v2.schema.json`. This is the contract-first move — graders (Wave 2) build against `Metrics`, and the coordinator/runtime (Wave 3) marshals it and validates against the schema.

Purpose: Make METRIC-01's acceptance ("schema validates; missing metrics are explicit nulls, not omissions") and D-06/D-07 (typed nullable fields + per-metric error annotation) machine-enforceable BEFORE any grader produces a value. Without this, every downstream grader would invent its own ad-hoc shape.

Output: `bench/evaluators/metrics.go` (`Metrics`, `MetricError`), an extended `result.v2.schema.json` (minor bump per Phase 75 policy), and TDD tests proving nil→null marshaling and additive-only schema compatibility.
</objective>
