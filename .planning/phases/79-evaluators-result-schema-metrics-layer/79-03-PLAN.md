<objective>
Implement the two trace-derived graders: `token_meter` (tokens_input/output + the two cached-token columns, sourced exclusively from the provider `usage` block) and `tool_trace_analyzer` (tool_calls, wall_time_seconds, files_read, bytes_read, lsp_diagnostics_used, semantic_tool_calls, retry_count — all derived from the already-merged `trace.MergedTrace`). Both REUSE the Phase 67/77 trace substrate (`internal/eval/trace`) rather than rebuilding it.

Purpose: This plan enforces the load-bearing METRIC-03 source-of-truth rule (tokens come from the provider response's `usage` block, never Helix's MCP-side counter — D-02) and the D-01 null-vs-zero honesty rule for the scripted corpus. It also satisfies the METRIC-06 reuse mandate: consume `RunCell`'s already-merged trace, never re-merge (which would re-parse the daemon log and risk divergent PID-gate behavior). TDD locks the null-vs-zero decision and the trace-derivations against fixtures before implementation.

Output: `bench/evaluators/token_meter/` (with a captured CC stream-json `testdata/` fixture carrying a real `usage` block) and `bench/evaluators/tool_trace_analyzer/`, each with RED→GREEN tests for the behaviors named in VALIDATION.md.
</objective>
